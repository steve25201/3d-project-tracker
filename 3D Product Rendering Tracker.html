Executive summary

Stephen Hany will create 20 original, concept-based 3D product models for OpenProject GmbH, each with realistic materials, professional studio lighting and high-quality 4K renders. The work is planned as a 7-day intensive schedule in five batches of four products, with client checkpoints on Day 1 and Day 5 and a final QA and delivery on Day 7.

Expected output: 20 completed models and at least 60 final 4K renders (3 per product), plus editable source files, texture files, material files and organized project folders.

Scope

Concept-based project. This is a concept-based visualization project, not reverse engineering or exact reproduction of existing commercial products. The products are original concepts based on their categories. No manufacturer CAD files, engineering drawings, orthographic blueprints or exact dimension sheets are provided.

Requirements

These apply to every product. Each product card in the Products section carries the full checklist.

Core modeling

  • Create a clean, professional 3D model
  • Establish believable and realistic proportions
  • Create logically constructed product forms
  • Maintain clean and organized topology
  • Keep the scene professionally organized
  • Add appropriate secondary details
  • Add realistic surface details where required
  • Ensure the model is suitable for high-quality rendering
  • Keep the model editable
  • Maintain consistent quality with the other products

Material & texture

  • Identify appropriate materials based on the product category
  • Create realistic material properties
  • Establish correct roughness / specular / reflection behavior
  • Add appropriate surface variation
  • Create realistic textures where necessary
  • Ensure materials hold up at 4K resolution
  • Keep materials properly organized
  • Ensure texture files are included with the final project

Studio lighting

  • Establish clean studio lighting
  • Create realistic highlights
  • Create believable shadows
  • Control reflections
  • Ensure the product is clearly readable
  • Maintain visual consistency between products
  • Avoid distracting backgrounds or lighting artifacts

Required angles

  • Front / ¾ Hero View
  • Rear / Side View
  • Close-up / Detail View

Minimum 3 renders per product. Additional angles may be created where needed to communicate the product.

Every render

  • 4K resolution (3840 × 2160)
  • PNG format
  • Clean white or light-gray studio background
  • Realistic shadows
  • Realistic reflections
  • Professional camera composition
  • Product clearly readable
  • No visible technical errors
  • Consistent presentation quality

Expected total output

  • 20 completed 3D product models
  • At least 20 × 3 = 60 final 4K renders
  • Editable source files
  • Blender / Maya / 3ds Max files as agreed
  • Texture files
  • Material files
  • Organized project folders
  • Final rendered images
  • Any additional agreed assets
